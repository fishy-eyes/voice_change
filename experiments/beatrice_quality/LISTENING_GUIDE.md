# Beatrice Quality Listening Guide

Use headphones at a comfortable, low level. Do not try to remember every file.
Start with only these three:

1. `outputs/01_native_offline.wav`
2. `outputs/02_streaming_QQ.wav`
3. `outputs/04_streaming_HQ.wav`

For each pair, answer only:

- Which is clearer: A, B, or about the same?
- Which has less electronic/metallic sound?
- Which sounds more natural?
- Which preserves long phrases better?

## 1. Native vs QQ vs HQ

First compare Native and QQ, then QQ and HQ. This decides whether streaming or
resampling is a priority suspect. Do not compare voice identity.

## 2. Source Pitch Range

Listen to `10_` through `12_`. Pay attention to high notes, low-note buzzing,
rapid intonation, sentence endings, and consonant-to-vowel transitions.

## 3. Pitch

Listen to `20_` through `22_`. Decide whether the center or a small ±2 semitone
change reduces electronic sound or improves intelligibility.

## 4. Formant

Listen to `30_` through `32_`. Focus on metallic tone, nasal tone, resonance,
and blurred consonants.

## 5. VQ Neighbors

Listen to `40_` through `42_`. Compare clarity, timbre stability, artifacts,
connected speech, and electronic sound.

## 6. Speakers

Listen to `50_` through `54_`. The question is whether Chinese blur/electronic
sound is common to all five JVS speakers or varies substantially by speaker.

Record the simple A/B answers before changing any production setting.
